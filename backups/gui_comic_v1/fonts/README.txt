Optional comic fonts for the GUI.

Put .ttf files here and the app uses them automatically:
  - Bangers-Regular.ttf      (titles, buttons, labels)  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bangers
  - ComicNeue-Bold.ttf       (small text)               https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Comic+Neue

Without them the app falls back to Impact and Comic Sans MS (built into Windows).
